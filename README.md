# Pawvis

Hands-free control for your Mac: point, pinch, and speak. Pawvis is a menu bar app
that turns your webcam into a gesture-controlled pointing device (with on-screen
fingertip overlays) and your voice into a keyboard (via OpenAI realtime
transcription). All hand tracking runs locally on-device; only dictation audio
leaves your machine, and only while you have dictation armed.
